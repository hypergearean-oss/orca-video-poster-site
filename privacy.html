Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary

Orca Video Poster runs on your own computer. We do not operate a server that receives your videos, Google data or sign-in credentials, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Google API access

To upload videos, the tool asks you to sign in with Google and grants the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube. The tool uses this access only to:

  • confirm which YouTube channel the signed-in account controls;
  • upload video files you choose, as private videos;
  • read back the privacy setting, processing status and channel of those videos.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The tool does not use Google user data for advertising, does not transfer it to third parties except as needed to operate the uploads you request, and does not allow humans to read it except where needed for security or legal reasons.

Credential storage

  • Your OAuth client secret file is kept on your computer, outside any project folder. It is never published or sent to us.
  • Access and refresh tokens are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, tied to your Windows user account, and stored in a per-channel folder on your computer.
  • Tokens are never written to logs, reports or the project's version-control history.

Data usage

The tool processes locally: the video file you select, its title and description, and the channel identity returned by Google. It keeps a local upload history per channel so duplicates can be detected. That history stays on your computer.

Data we receive

We receive nothing from the tool. There are no analytics, tracking or telemetry in the upload workflow.

Deleting your data

  • Local data: delete the channel folders under your .orca\youtube folder. This removes the saved tokens, channel pins and upload history.
  • Google access: revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uploaded videos: delete them in YouTube Studio. Deleting local data does not delete videos from YouTube.

Questions

Open an issue at the project's GitHub repository: github.com/hypergearean-oss/orca-video-poster-site/issues.

Orca Video Poster is an independent project, not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
